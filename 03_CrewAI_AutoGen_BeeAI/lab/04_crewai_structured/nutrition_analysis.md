### Suggestions for Healthier Alternatives and Budget Adjustments

1. **Carrot Ginger Dressing:**
   - **Homemade Alternative:** Create a homemade dressing using fresh carrots, ginger, lemon juice, and a small amount of olive oil. This can significantly reduce costs and control added sugars and fats.

2. **Cherry Tomatoes:**
   - **Substitution:** Use regular tomatoes or canned diced tomatoes if they are more affordable. This can help reduce the overall cost.

3. **Quinoa:**
   - **Bulk Purchase:** Buy quinoa in bulk or opt for store brands to save money.

4. **Chickpeas:**
   - **Dry Chickpeas:** Consider using dry chickpeas instead of canned. They are cheaper and can be cooked in bulk.

5. **Vegetables:**
   - **Frozen Alternatives:** Use frozen butternut squash and peas, which are often cheaper and have a longer shelf life.

### Budget-Friendly Meal Plan
By making the dressing at home and opting for frozen or bulk alternatives, you can reduce the cost and stay within the $20 budget. This approach not only saves money but also allows for better control over the nutritional content of the meal.
